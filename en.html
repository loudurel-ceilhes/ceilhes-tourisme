Welcome

Welcome to Ceilhes-et-Rocozels

A small village of the Haut-Languedoc, in the upper Orb valley (Hérault, southern France) — 430 m up, wrapped in forests, between a big quiet lake and a mountain river. About 1h15 from Montpellier or Béziers, 20 minutes off the A75 motorway.

Getting to the village

By train · station 4 km from the centre

Ceilhes-Roqueredonde station is in the neighbouring municipality of Roqueredonde. It has no ticket office or staff: buy your ticket before travelling. Check trains and possible replacement buses on SNCF Connect.

Arrange the last 4 km in advance. Speak to your host or book a taxi transfer (FR); the station is not in the village centre.

By bus · liO line 688

Line 688 links Le Bousquet-d’Orb to central Ceilhes via Avène-les-Bains and Rocozels, Monday to Friday. Check the actual timetable and your return journey on Hérault Transport.

For connections and other travel information, see the bus information (FR).

By car · from the A75

The village is about 20 minutes from the A75, a little over an hour from Montpellier or Béziers, and around 45 minutes from Millau.

Find fuel information (FR) and electric vehicle charging information (FR).

Explore the area

🏊 The lake & swimming

The 6-km Lac d'Avène is a setting for canoeing and paddle-boarding. Le Bouloc, at the village entrance, has playgrounds, picnic areas and fishing pontoons. Swimming at Le Bouloc and the lake is not supervised. Check current water-quality results, local notices and permitted areas before entering the water; an older rating does not establish today's conditions. 🏞 The Orb river

The Orb runs through the village, with a ford and natural rock pools. Enjoy the river from the bank, especially with children. Water levels and currents can change after rain; clear, cool water does not guarantee safe swimming. Check local restrictions and conditions before entering.
